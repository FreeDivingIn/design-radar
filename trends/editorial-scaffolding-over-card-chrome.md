# Editorial scaffolding carries grouping

Status: `emerging`

## Observation

A current batch of expressive brand and portfolio sites makes page structure legible through typography, spacing, grids, labels, indices and sequence, while persistent bordered or rounded card surfaces play a smaller structural role.

- **Arc’teryx System 0** uses a living identity built on a dynamic grid and flexible layouts. The experience translates a complex circular system into staged, editorial communication rather than a repeated card stack.
- **ANDMADE** exposes structure through large statements, case counts, dated entries, a `Txt / Img` viewing switch and even a grid-reveal shortcut. Hierarchy is carried by scale, rhythm and the underlying grid.
- **Studio Loop** uses repeated year/location labels, numbered service sections, oversized language and alternating project/media sequences to establish hierarchy without making every group a surfaced container.

The shared mechanism is not “no borders.” Grouping remains visible, but more of that work is assigned to editorial scaffolding.

## Inference

On low-to-moderate-density expressive websites, designers appear more willing to let spatial and typographic relationships define object boundaries. This leaves more visual weight available for imagery, identity and motion, and reduces the sense that every content unit must become a component-shaped card.

## Fit

Brand sites, launches, editorial stories and portfolios where browsing, identity and narrative matter as much as fast object comparison.

## Avoid

Dense enterprise, commerce or data workflows where persistent boundaries communicate selection, state, ownership, drag targets, comparison units or other operational affordances. “Remove cards” is not a valid rule by itself.

## Evidence

- [Arc’teryx System 0 — forpeople](https://www.forpeople.com/projects/arcteryx-system0-circularity/) — direct description of a dynamic grid, flexible layouts and a simplified guest journey.
- [ANDMADE](https://andmade.jp/) — live structure includes case counts, dated entries and `Txt / Img` modes; its About page exposes the underlying grid.
- [Studio Loop](https://www.studioloop.com.br/) — live site uses numbered services, large typographic sections and repeated temporal/project sequences.
- [Site of Sites](https://www.siteofsites.co/) — September 2026 curation independently surfaces both System 0 and ANDMADE.
- [Lapa Ninja](https://www.lapa.ninja/) — current latest-web batch independently surfaces Studio Loop.

## Uncertainty

Evidence is strongest for expressive websites, not application UI. The signal should stay scoped there until later batches show the same mechanism in task-oriented interfaces.
