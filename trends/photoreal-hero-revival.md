# Photoreal hero imagery returns, restaged

Status: `emerging` (Revival)

## What returned

Photographic hero imagery — a widely documented web-design default of the mid-2010s full-screen hero era — is again the dominant imagery vocabulary among newly curated sites. Among illustration-tagged sites added July 2025 – August 2026, `Image` share reached 84.1%, versus 53.4% in the June 2025 baseline batch, while rendered 3D and vector illustration fell away over the same batches.

## What changed

The returned photography is restaged rather than nostalgic. Verified current instances use photoreal scenes — plausibly CG- or AI-produced — as atmospheric backdrops composited with real product UI and type: Prime Intellect sets a photoreal landscape scene behind its interface panel, Conductor combines atmospheric imagery with product UI, and Mckp.live presents photoreal device scenes as part of the product demonstration. This is not the older pattern of one stock photograph carrying the whole hero; imagery now shares the stage with the product artifact.

## Fit

Product, AI and dev-tool heroes that want realism and atmosphere without relying on a pure illustration or 3D-render vocabulary.

## Avoid

Reading this as stock-photo nostalgia or as license for generic imagery; category mix carries part of the move, and recurrence was first detected through one curated stream.

## Evidence

- [Prime Intellect](https://www.primeintellect.ai/), [Conductor](https://www.conductor.build/), [Mckp.live](https://mckp.live/) — independent current sites, verified against their original live pages.
- [Creative Bloq: 10 website trends that defined 2015](https://www.creativebloq.com/netmag/10-website-trends-defined-2015-111517948) — contemporaneous documentation that full-background photographic hero imagery was a dominant 2015 web pattern.
- Dataset measurement and batch comparison as documented in `trends/photography-over-rendered-imagery.md`.

## Uncertainty

The historical anchor is now externally corroborated and the current instances are verified at their original sites. The recurrence signal was still discovered through one curated stream, so keep this `emerging` until independent discovery sources repeat the same restaged pattern. This remains a complementary temporal reading of `photography-over-rendered-imagery`: the current lane tracks vocabulary displacement; this entry tracks the historical return and its changed staging.
