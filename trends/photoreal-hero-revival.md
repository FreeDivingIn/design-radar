# Photoreal hero imagery returns, restaged

Status: `emerging` (Revival)

## What returned

Photographic hero imagery — the default of the early-2010s full-bleed photo era — is again the dominant imagery vocabulary among newly curated sites. Among illustration-tagged sites added July 2025 – August 2026, `Image` share reached 84.1%, versus 53.4% in the June 2025 baseline batch, while rendered 3D and vector illustration fell away over the same batches.

## What changed

The returned photography is restaged rather than nostalgic. Verified current instances use photoreal scenes — plausibly CG- or AI-produced — as atmospheric backdrops composited with real product UI and type: Primeintellect sets a photoreal landscape scene behind its interface panel, Conductor lays a photographic sky texture under product UI, Mckp.live presents a photoreal device scene on a dark ground. This is not full-bleed stock photography carrying the whole hero; the photograph now shares the stage with the product.

## Fit

Product, AI and dev-tool heroes that want realism and atmosphere without commissioning 3D render pipelines.

## Avoid

Reading this as stock-photo nostalgia or as license for generic imagery; category mix carries part of the move, and the evidence is one curated stream.

## Evidence

- [Primeintellect](https://www.primeintellect.ai/), [Conductor](https://www.conductor.build/), [Mckp.live](https://mckp.live/) — covers visually verified 2026-09-10.
- Dataset measurement and batch comparison as documented in `trends/photography-over-rendered-imagery.md`.

## Uncertainty

The historical anchor (early-2010s photo-hero era) is design-history knowledge, not dataset-internal evidence. Recurrence evidence is single-source. This is a complementary temporal reading of the same shift tracked by the current-lane signal `photography-over-rendered-imagery` — the current lane tracks vocabulary displacement, this entry records the historical return and what changed in staging.
