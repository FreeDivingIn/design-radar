# Controlled irregularity interrupts polished systems

Status: `emerging`

## Observation

Several recent identities introduce non-uniformity as a bounded part of an otherwise controlled system rather than adding a generic rough texture afterward.

- **Manta Chocolate** places a handmade logo gesture deliberately outside a rigorous information structure; organic forms and texture supply tactility around highly functional ingredient communication.
- **Runblock** builds heat pressure into the wordmark itself—curves swell and merge—and uses motion that is intentionally reactive and slightly irregular rather than mechanically smooth.
- **Phillips Print** pairs a central wordmark with interchangeable marks and develops the identity through repeated physical printing, allowing real production to shape the system.
- **Instagram’s 2026 refresh** is framed around everyday making and a broad range of personal expression rather than one immaculate visual mood.

## Inference

A small but repeated shift is visible from uniform polish toward controlled irregularity that exposes gesture, process or physical response. The useful variable is not “make it messy”; it is where a system deliberately permits variation and why.

## Fit

Creative, food, culture, sport and community brands where tactility, authorship or physical process contributes to meaning.

## Avoid

Interfaces where irregularity reduces legibility, state clarity or trust; brands with no authentic process to support it; cosmetic distress added only to simulate “human” work.

- [Phillips Print — Laura Clink / Behance](https://www.behance.net/gallery/245329283/Phillips-Print) — first-party project presents interchangeable marks and print-derived visual language.
- [Instagram refresh — It’s Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/behind-instagrams-first-major-refresh-in-10-years-partnership-130826) — documents the broader system and its emphasis on varied everyday expression.

## Uncertainty

Most evidence is concentrated in August–September. Keep `emerging` until another independent time batch repeats the mechanism; do not treat imperfection itself as a quality heuristic.
