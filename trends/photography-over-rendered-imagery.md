# Photography displaces rendered and vector imagery

Status: `emerging`

## Observation

In a structured dataset of 2,211 curated sites (single curated stream), sites added between July 2025 and August 2026 differ sharply from the June 2025 baseline in imagery vocabulary. Among illustration-tagged sites, `Image` share rose 53.4% → 84.1% (+30.7pp; 886 → 214 tagged sites), while `3D` fell 33.3% → 12.6% and `Vector` fell 46.2% → 20.6%. Hero layout distribution stayed flat across the same batches, so the shift sits in imagery vocabulary rather than page structure.

- **Primeintellect** stages a photoreal landscape scene as its hero backdrop.
- **Conductor** sets a photographic sky texture behind real product UI.
- **Mckp.live** presents a photoreal device scene on a dark ground.

## Inference

Photographic and photoreal imagery is retaking ground that rendered 3D scenes and vector illustration held as the default vocabulary a year earlier. The effect survives controlling for tag-coverage drift between batches, which rules out a pure tagging artifact; but evidence comes from one curated stream, so curation-taste drift cannot be excluded.

## Fit

Hero and section imagery direction for product, AI and dev-tool sites; choosing photography over commissioning 3D renders.

## Avoid

Treating photography as mandatory. Part of the move is carried by category mix (more AI and productivity launches), and illustration tags are site-level — some tagged sites remain type-led in the hero.

## Evidence

- [Primeintellect](https://www.primeintellect.ai/) — photoreal landscape hero; surfaced August 2026.
- [Conductor](https://www.conductor.build/) — photographic sky texture with real UI; surfaced August 2026.
- [Mckp.live](https://mckp.live/) — photoreal device scene; surfaced August 2026.
- Batch comparison over the 2,211-site dataset (baseline n=1,946 vs additions n=265; 65 pre-registered tests, BH-corrected); instance covers visually verified 2026-09-10.

## Uncertainty

Single source; needs at least one independent source with fresh instances before status can advance. A dual-batch comparison cannot by itself support `growing`.
