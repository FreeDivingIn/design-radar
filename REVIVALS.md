# Revivals

Historical design languages that are becoming newly relevant in contemporary work.

A revival requires both:

- **Historical anchor** — a recognizable prior design language, movement, period, or visual convention.
- **Current recurrence** — multiple independent contemporary projects are reactivating it now.

For each revival, record both **what returned** and **what changed**. A revival can be strong at the creative frontier before it reaches broad production adoption.

## Photoreal hero imagery returns, restaged — `emerging`
**What returned:** photographic hero imagery, the early-2010s full-bleed default, again dominates new-site vocabulary — Image share 53.4% → 84.1% among illustration-tagged additions since July 2025. **What changed:** photoreal scenes, plausibly CG/AI-produced, now act as atmospheric backdrops composited with real product UI rather than stock photos carrying the whole hero (Primeintellect, Conductor, Mckp.live). **Fit:** product/AI/dev-tool heroes wanting realism without render pipelines. **Avoid:** stock-photo nostalgia; single curated source. → `trends/photoreal-hero-revival.md`
