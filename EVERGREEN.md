# Evergreen

Long-lived design mechanisms that remain useful after their novelty or trend momentum fades.

This lane is not a popularity archive. Promote a pattern here only when it has demonstrated:

- **Longevity** — it remains useful across a substantial time span.
- **Transferability** — it works across multiple products, brands, media, or contexts.
- **Utility** — it solves a stable design problem rather than merely repeating a recognizable visual feature.

Evergreen entries should describe durable relationships or mechanisms, not just long-lived stylistic features.

## Editorial scaffolding carries grouping — `established`
**Mechanism:** grouping carried by type scale, spacing, grids, labels, indices and sequence instead of persistent surfaced containers. **Longevity:** editorial mechanism predating the web; verified currently stable on expressive sites (System 0, ANDMADE, Studio Loop across September batches). **Transferability:** brand, editorial, launch and portfolio contexts. **Utility:** spatial/typographic grouping without forcing every unit into a card at low-to-moderate density. **Avoid:** dense transactional/data UI. → `trends/editorial-scaffolding-over-card-chrome.md`

## Split hero pairs message with proof — `established`
**Mechanism:** two-column hero pairing a message block with visual evidence in one viewport. **Longevity:** 51.3% prevalence in a 2,211-site curated dataset and statistically flat across 14 months of new additions (50.1% → 44.5%, ns) while surface vocabularies shifted 20–40pp. **Transferability:** 42–66% share across 15 of 16 site categories. **Utility:** delivers claim and demonstration simultaneously above the fold. **Avoid:** pure brand/atmosphere pieces; splits without a real proof asset. → `trends/split-hero-message-and-proof.md`

## Top-bar navigation carries orientation — `established`
**Mechanism:** identity, wayfinding and primary action held in one conventional top row. **Longevity:** 94.5% prevalence and exactly batch-stable (93.5% → 93.6%). **Transferability:** ≥86.7% in every one of 16 categories. **Utility:** persistent orientation at near-zero learning cost; convention arrives pre-formed. **Avoid:** reading as "never innovate nav" — the value is orientation, not the bar. → `trends/top-bar-navigation.md`

## CTA weight scales with task orientation — `established`
**Mechanism:** explicit button affordance whose visual weight tracks visitor intent frequency. **Longevity:** 81.8% prevalence, stable across batches (77.3% → 82.2%, ns). **Transferability:** share grades systematically from task contexts (web apps 97.2%, design tools 95.6%) to expressive ones (portfolio 56.6%), with text links taking over where tone matters. **Utility:** maximum affordance for ready intent without paying pushiness costs in browse contexts. **Avoid:** max-strength CTA in editorial contexts; hidden primary action in task flows. → `trends/context-graded-cta-button.md`
